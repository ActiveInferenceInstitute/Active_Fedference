# Research audits and evidence boundaries

This section indexes the source-owned research reviews that bound the project's
scientific, statistical, visual, computational, and implementation claims.
Dated reviews preserve the scope and evidence available at their stated review
date; they do not by themselves establish that the current generated outputs or
public release are source-current. Start with the claim ledgers, then follow the
specialized audits relevant to the change under review.

## Claim and scholarship ledgers

- [`manuscript-claim-audit.md`](manuscript-claim-audit.md) — manuscript-wide
  claim status and the formal, conditional, source-conditional, and open lanes.
- [`visual-claim-audit.md`](visual-claim-audit.md) — figure estimands,
