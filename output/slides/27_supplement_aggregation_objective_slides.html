Why the sharp heuristic is not yet variational

The sharp server rule is empirically strong, but this repository has not established a variational certificate for it.

Why the sharp heuristic is… (part 2)

The heuristic of Equation (10) alternates a reverse-KL weight update \(a_n \leftarrow w_n\exp(-c\,\mathrm{KL}(q_n \,\|\, q))\).

Why the sharp heuristic is… (part 3)

It couples that update with the log-linear consensus \(q \leftarrow \mathrm{softmax}(\sum_n a_n \log q_n)\). For the natural direct objective \(\sum_n a_n \mathrm{KL}(q_n \,\|\, q) + \tfrac{1}{c}\mathrm{KL_{gen}}(a\,\|\,w)\), the reverse-KL rule is the \(a\)-minimizer.

Aggregation free energy and its block minimizers

Definition (Aggregation free energy)

For \(c,\lambda>0\), consensus \(q\), and nonnegative effective weights \(a=(a_n)\), define \(F_\lambda(q,a)\) by (12), where \(\mathrm{CE}(q,q_n)=-\sum_i q_i\log q_{n,i}\), \(H(q)\) is entropy, and \(\mathrm{KL}_{\rm gen}(a\|w)=\sum_n g_n\) for \(g_n=a_n\log(a_n/w_n)-a_n+w_n\).

Aggregation free energy… (part 2)

The \(q\)-block. For \(\lambda>0\), fixing \(a\), the \(q\)-dependent part of \(F_\lambda\) is denoted \(J(q;a)\) and can be written in either of two equivalent forms:

\[ \begin{aligned} J(q;a) &=\sum_n a_n\,\mathrm{CE}(q,q_n)-\lambda H(q),\\ J(q;a) &=\lambda\sum_i q_i\log q_i\\ &\quad-\sum_i\sum_n q_i a_n\log q_{n,i}. \end{aligned} \]

Adding a Lagrange multiplier to the block in Equation (27) for \(\sum_i q_i = 1\) and differentiating gives \(\lambda\log q_i + \lambda - \sum_n a_n \log q_{n,i} + \mu = 0\), i.e.

Aggregation free energy… (part 10)

Each block update is the exact minimizer of its block, so alternating them is block-coordinate descent: \(F\) is non-increasing at every half-step. When the iterates converge, their fixed point is coordinatewise stationary.

Conservative-server properties

Theorem (Properties)

For \(c,\lambda>0\), (28)–(29) make \(F\) non-increasing. At convergence, \((q^*,a^*)\) is coordinatewise stationary. As \(c\to0\), \(a_n\to w_n\) and \(q\) tends to the tempered log-linear pool; \(\lambda=1.0\) gives (9). Finally, \(a_n=w_n\exp[-c\,\mathrm{CE}(q,q_n)]\le w_n\), and \(\mathrm{KL}(q\,\|\,q_n)\to\infty\) gives \(a_n\to0\).

Tempered aggregation family for the accuracy-guarantee trade

The aggregator of Section 12.2 fixes the entropy term at unit weight. Relaxing that single coefficient generates a one-parameter tempered family. Introduce an entropy weight \(\lambda > 0\) —

Tempered aggregation… (part 2)

Tempered aggregation… (part 3)

\[ \begin{aligned} F_\lambda(q, a) &= \sum_n a_n\,\mathrm{CE}(q, q_n) - \lambda H(q)\\ &\quad + \tfrac{1}{c}\,\mathrm{KL_{gen}}(a \,\|\, w). \end{aligned} \]

Tempered aggregation… (part 4)

Repeating the \(q\)-block derivation of Equation (28) with the entropy scaled by \(\lambda\) leaves the \(a\)-block untouched and tempers only the consensus update:

Tempered aggregation… (part 5)

\[ \begin{aligned} q &\propto \exp\!\Big( \tfrac{1}{\lambda}\textstyle\sum_n a_n \log q_n\Big),\\ a_n &= w_n\,\exp\!\big(-c\,\mathrm{CE}(q, q_n)\big). \end{aligned} \]

Tempered aggregation… (part 6)

The \(\lambda\downarrow0\) endpoint is separately implemented as a deterministic tied-argmax rule; it is not obtained by substituting \(\lambda=0\) into Equation (30) or Equation (31).

Tempered aggregation… (part 7)

The weight update is independent of \(\lambda\): the bound \(a_n \le w_n\) with collapse \(a_n \to 0\) as \(\mathrm{KL}(q\,\|\,q_n) \to \infty\) is unchanged, so the raw effective-weight bound of Section 12.3 holds for every \(\lambda > 0\).

Tempered aggregation… (part 8)

At \(\lambda = 1.0\) the temperature is unity and Equation (31) is identical to the current axis-3 aggregator Equation (13) — the default is bit-identical, not merely close.
