Contamination sweep (part 2)

Variational aggregator: conservative objective-backed weight control

The heuristic has the higher point accuracy in the configured verdict cell. The variational aggregator of Section 5.8.2, derived in Section 12, is its objective-backed complement.

Variational aggregator (part 2)

Each exact block update does not increase the stated free energy Equation (12), and a converged fixed point is coordinatewise stationary. Two deterministic runs show its weight behavior at robustness 1.50.

Variational aggregator (part 3)

First, the descent. On a contaminated colony the free energy falls monotonically from 3.2458 to 2.3780 (a drop of 0.8678 over 11 block-coordinate iterations, converged: Yes), with a largest single-step increase of 8.88e-16 — machine zero, the numerical witness of the descent theorem (Section 12.3).

Variational aggregator (part 17)

The variational aggregator therefore does not win the peak-accuracy verdict of Section 7.5.2 — that remains the sharp heuristic’s role — and the two are reported as complements, never conflated: rigor-with-conservatism on one side, accuracy-without-an-objective on the other.
