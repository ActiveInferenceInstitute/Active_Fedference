Aggregation and message… (part 2)

The server step lives in aggregation.py, where a categorical specialization of the active-inference belief-sharing relation (Friston et al. 2024) and the FedGVI objective (Mildner et al. 2025) meet. Each agent \(n\) broadcasts a categorical local posterior \(q_n(s)\) over the shared latent factor,

Aggregation and message… (part 3)

optionally with a scalar base weight \(w_n\).

Two fusion rules act directly on these broadcasts, and a third — the objective-backed variational_aggregate of Section 5.8.2 — refines the second into descent on a stated objective.

Aggregation and message… (part 4)

The first is the log-linear pool, a project-local product-of-experts consensus. In the terminology of opinion pooling it is the logarithmic pool,

Aggregation and message… (part 5)

a weighted geometric aggregation rule whose Bayesian-coherence assumptions have been studied independently of active inference (Genest and Zidek 1986; Genest et al. 1986; Carvalho et al. 2023); in machine-learning terms it is the product-of-experts normalization of local posteriors (Hinton 2002):

Aggregation and message… (part 6)

\[ \begin{aligned} &\operatorname{log\_linear\_pool}(\{q_n\})\\ &\qquad = \mathrm{softmax}\!\Big(\textstyle\sum_n w_n \log q_n\Big). \end{aligned} \]

Aggregation and message… (part 7)

In the floating-point implementation, \(q_n\) denotes the admitted posterior after input validation, lower flooring of masses, and row normalization. The floor acts before normalization and applies to small positive masses as well as exact zeros. The pooling formula uses those admitted posteriors;

Aggregation and message… (part 8)

rescaling sufficiently small raw masses can change them.

For the source bridge, fix one finite shared support \(\mathcal S\) with \(q_n(s)>0\) for every agent and state.

Aggregation and message… (part 9)

Suppose the inputs to Eq. 7’s softmax message-combination term can be represented as posterior log potentials \(m_n(s)=\log q_n(s)+\kappa_n\), where \(\kappa_n\) is constant in \(s\), and use declared fixed weights \(w_n\) that do not depend on the emerging consensus (the unweighted case sets each \(w_n=1\)).

Aggregation and message… (part 10)

Additive constants then cancel under softmax, giving exactly Equation (9).

Aggregation and message… (part 11)

This is a categorical posterior-log-potential specialization of the source equation’s message-combination term, not a reconstruction of source message construction, self-exclusion/cavity policy, scheduling, generative factors, or the complete protocol.

Aggregation and message… (part 12)

The code alias friston_belief_share names this qualified specialization only.

Aggregation and message… (part 13)

The second rule is robust_aggregate, an iteratively-reweighted pool that discounts each agent by \(\exp(-c\,\mathrm{KL}(q_n \,\|\, q))\) against the emerging consensus \(q\). A confidently-wrong (contaminated) agent sits far from the consensus,

Aggregation and message… (part 14)

can earn a small effective weight and be suppressed in the declared diagnostic regimes.

Aggregation and message… (part 15)

This independently motivated rule does not transfer FedGVI’s client theorem to the server side: it is the heuristic robustness axis of Section 3.3, distinct from the per-agent rigorous axis of Section 5.7.

Aggregation and message… (part 16)

It is also only an analogy to robust federated aggregation methods such as divergence-weighted gamma-mean aggregation, geometric-median robust aggregation, or Byzantine-tolerant gradient aggregation (Li et al. 2022; Pillutla et al. 2022; Blanchard et al. 2017).

Aggregation and message… (part 17)

Those methods motivate the risk surface, but they do not supply this rule’s guarantee.

The defining identity is bit-level: at zero robustness the reweighted pool is the log-linear pool unchanged.

\[ \operatorname{robust\_aggregate}(0) \;\equiv\; \operatorname{log\_linear\_pool}. \]

This is an exact project-local code identity. Under the stated posterior-log-potential assumptions, its right-hand side specializes the message-combination term of Eq. 7; the identity itself neither recovers nor certifies the complete source protocol (Friston et al. 2024).

Variational aggregation with objective-backed weight control

The related server construction becomes a genuinely variational rule by replacing the heuristic’s reverse-KL weight update with a forward cross-entropy update. For \(c>0\) and \(\lambda>0\),

Variational aggregation with… (part 2)

treat the consensus \(q\) and a vector of effective weights \(a = (a_n)\) as joint variational parameters and define the aggregation free energy

Variational aggregation with… (part 3)

\[ \begin{aligned} F_\lambda(q, a) &= \sum_n a_n\,\mathrm{CE}(q, q_n) - \lambda H(q)\\ &\quad + \tfrac{1}{c}\,\mathrm{KL_{gen}}(a \,\|\, w). \end{aligned} \]

Variational aggregation with… (part 4)

where \(\mathrm{CE}(q, q_n) = -\sum_i q_i \log q_{n,i}\) is the cross-entropy of the consensus relative to agent \(n\), \(H(q)\) is the consensus entropy, \(c\) is the robustness, and \(\lambda>0\) is the entropy_weight coefficient (default \(\lambda=1.0\));

Variational aggregation with… (part 5)

\(\mathrm{KL_{gen}}(a \,\|\, w) = \sum_n [a_n \log(a_n/w_n) - a_n + w_n]\) is the generalized KL between the effective and base weights.

Each block of \(F_\lambda\) has a closed-form minimizer, so alternating

Variational aggregation with… (part 6)

\[ \begin{aligned} q &\leftarrow \mathrm{softmax}\!\Big( \tfrac{1}{\lambda}\textstyle\sum_n a_n \log q_n\Big),\\ a_n &\leftarrow w_n\,\exp\!\big(-c\,\mathrm{CE}(q, q_n)\big). \end{aligned} \]

Variational aggregation with… (part 7)

is exact block-coordinate descent on Equation (12) (variational_aggregate) for \(c>0\) and \(\lambda>0\).

Variational aggregation with… (part 8)

The implementation defines the \(\lambda\downarrow0\) endpoint separately as a deterministic tied-argmax rule; \(\lambda=0\) is not substituted into the objective or its \(q\)-update.

Variational aggregation with… (part 9)

This substitution changes both orientation and scale: \(\mathrm{CE}(q,q_n)=\mathrm{KL}(q\|q_n)+H(q)\), and its common \(H(q)\) term scales all raw weights, which changes the entropy of the subsequent unnormalized weighted log pool.

Variational aggregation with… (part 10)

The paired \(q\)- and \(a\)-updates in Equation (13) are exact block minimizers of the stated objective; that fact does not derive the reverse-KL heuristic.

Variational aggregation with… (part 11)

Because \(F\) is biconvex, we run the descent multi-start (pool, uniform, and arithmetic-mean seeds, lowest observed \(F\) among converged starts;

Variational aggregation with… (part 12)

otherwise the lowest unfinished trace is returned with converged=False) so a near-one-hot adversary is not left at the product-of-experts seed in the tested contamination regimes — the detail that supports the effective-weight diagnostic (Section 12).

This upgrades the server side from an untracked heuristic to a derived generalized-Bayes aggregation with an explicit redescending raw-weight property: a single confidently-wrong agent earns raw weight \(a_n = w_n\exp(-c\,\mathrm{CE}(q,q_n)) \le w_n\) that vanishes as it diverges,

Variational aggregation with… (part 17)

whereas the naive pool grants every agent the fixed weight \(w_n\) however wrong it is.

Variational aggregation with… (part 18)

The trade is conservatism — the \(-H(q)\) term makes the stationary point a maximum-entropy-biased consensus consistent with the weighted cross-entropies, so variational_aggregate is deliberately flatter than the product-of-experts and does not maximize peak point-accuracy.

The two server-side rules therefore play complementary, never-conflated roles, both reported:

Variational aggregation… (part 20)

the sharp robust_aggregate heuristic for accuracy under contamination (Section 7.5.2) and the conservative variational_aggregate for a server-side objective with stated weight control (Section 7.5.3).

Variational aggregation with… (part 21)

A temperature parameter \(\lambda>0\) (controlled by entropy_weight, default \(\lambda = 1.0\)) generalizes the objective to \(F_\lambda\); lower \(\lambda\) sharpens the variational \(q\)-block toward a maximizing state for its current weighted log pool.

Variational aggregation… (part 22)

The tempered family (Section 12.5; objective Equation (30)) recovers the full-entropy variational aggregator at \(\lambda = 1.0\) and has a separately implemented deterministic tied-argmax endpoint as \(\lambda\downarrow0\);

Variational aggregation… (part 23)

The effective-weight \(a\)-update is unchanged for every \(\lambda>0\), so the raw-weight bound holds over the objective-defined family.
