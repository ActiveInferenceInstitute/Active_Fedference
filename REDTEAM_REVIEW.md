## Parallel-analysis conclusion

The strongest steelman is that 240 primary seeds, 480 headline matched trials,
and a 64-seed structural tier are materially more precise than a single
illustrative run, with MCSE/MDE and bootstrap intervals surfaced. The strongest
counterargument is that 480 trials at one fixed world are not 480 independent
worlds, and large conditional samples can make tiny mechanistic differences look
decisive. The deciding assumption is therefore the declared estimand, not the
raw count. The repository now states that assumption and reduces nested trials
before seed-level summaries.

## Claim Boundaries

- **Client-side FedGVI axis:** rigorous per-client generalized-Bayes updates
  (`rcce`, beta loss, AR/KL limits). The logistic-regression robustness figure is
  a small, token-backed anchor with seed-level intervals; it is not described as
  a full GPU-scale FedGVI BNN reproduction.
- **Sharp server axis:** `robust_aggregate` is a useful heuristic with a tested
  recovery limit only. No bounded-influence guarantee is transferred to it.
- **Objective-backed server axis:** `variational_aggregate` carries a proven
  raw effective-weight bound with empirical redescending behavior — not an
  estimator-level bounded-influence (B-robustness) proof for the normalized
  consensus — and is reported as conservative rather than as the peak-accuracy
